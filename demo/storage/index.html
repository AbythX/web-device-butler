Several tabs at once

Press the same button in two tabs at the same moment. update() counts every step of both tabs; reading and then setting loses steps, because each tab overwrites what the other one just wrote.

Streams

Large data is written and read in pieces, without holding it in memory. Only opfs and memory can do that; on the other drivers the calls fail with a StorageUnsupportedError.
