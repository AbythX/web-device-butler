web-device-butler

Every page runs on the source of the library. Nothing is requested from the browser before you press a button. Most pages show more when they are open in two tabs at once.

Use cases

Small applications, each built the way docs/use-cases describes it.

  • Document scanner

    A back camera with torch and zoom, photos kept in the storage of the browser.

  • Voice memos

    A microphone with a level display, recorded straight into the storage as a stream.

  • Video message

    Camera and microphone as one session that another tab cannot take away while it records.

  • Settings in several tabs

    Settings and a counter that stay correct when several tabs change them at once.

One area each

Every call of an area behind a button, with the log of the library on the page.

  • Cameras

    What the library finds out about every camera. The tool for the device matrix.

  • Microphones

    Selection, processing, level and muting. The tool for the device matrix of the microphones.

  • Storage

    The drivers, entries, concurrent updates, locks, streams and the space of the origin.

  • Location, orientation, permissions

    Positions, a watch, how the device is held and the state of every permission.
